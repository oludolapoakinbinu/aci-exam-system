RULES
1. EVERY question must be verified. Do NOT trust answer keys blindly - several are known to be wrong.
   - Calculations: actually compute the answer with python
     (/Users/dolapo/aci-exam-system/.venv/bin/python -c "...") using ACI conventions
     (money market ACT/360 for most currencies, ACT/365 for GBP, AUD, NZD, HKD, SGD, ZAR, ...;
     bond conventions as stated; forward points = spot * (rv*d/basis_v - rb*d/basis_b)/(1+rb*d/basis_b), etc.).
     If none of the 4 options matches your computation, pick the nearest one ONLY if the gap is
     rounding; otherwise fix the question (adjust one option to the correct value) and note it.
   - Theory: confirm against the course material in source_text (grep it). Where the material and
     a key disagree, the material + current market practice win. Note: EONIA was replaced by €STR
     (Jan 2022) and LIBOR has been replaced by RFRs (SOFR, SONIA, €STR, SARON, TONA) - questions
     that only make sense historically may be kept but say so in the explanation.
2. Mark key_corrected=true with a correction_note whenever your answer differs from the source key.
3. Ignore OCR artefacts ("CS CamScanner", page numbers, headers). Repair obviously garbled OCR text.
4. If a source question is genuinely broken (missing data, two correct answers, unrecoverable),
   either repair it minimally so it is sound, or skip it - never emit an unsound question.
5. Options must be distinct. Keep the original option order (so answer keys line up) unless you repair.
6. Explanations are what students read after the exam. Make them clear, correct and short.
7. ORIGINAL questions (when asked): exam-style 4-option MCQs, a single unambiguous answer,
   plausible distractors (common mistakes: wrong day basis, bid/offer swapped, sign of points, etc.),
   spread across the subtopics of your material, with a mix of theory/calculation and difficulty.
   Randomise the position of the correct answer. source = "Original - based on <document/section>".
